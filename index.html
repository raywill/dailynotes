The Philosophy

Let It Flow.

Stop organizing. Stop categorizing. Just write. DailyNotes flows like a stream.
Each day is a fresh page.

Stream, Not Library

Friction kills the habit. We spend more time deciding "where does this go?" than writing. DailyNotes removes this barrier completely.

No Folders

Yesterday is yesterday. Today is today. Temporal organization is effortless and automatic.

Zero Mental Overhead

Just open and write. No "Should this be in Projects?". The lowest barrier to capture means you'll actually capture everything.

Tags Emerge Naturally

Organization is inverted. Use inline tags (#todo, #idea) as you write. Structure emerges from your workflow, not before it.

Trust Search

Your brain remembers context ("it was last Tuesday"). You don't need perfect organization—you need perfect retrieval.

Embrace Impermanence

Most notes are scaffolding. Let irrelevant content fade into the archive. It's there if you need it, but not cluttering your mind.
