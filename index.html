The Philosophy: Let It Flow

The best note-taking system is one you actually use

🌊 Notes Are a Stream, Not a Library

DailyNotes was born from a simple realization: the friction of organizing notes kills the habit of taking them. We spend more time deciding "where does this go?" than actually capturing thoughts.

No Folders, No Categories, No Decisions

Every day starts with a blank page. Yesterday's thoughts are yesterday. Today is today. This temporal organization is the only structure you need — it's automatic, effortless, and mirrors how life actually flows.

Zero Mental Overhead

Open DailyNotes. Write. That's it. No "Should this be in Projects or Personal?" No "Do I create a new folder?" No "Let me reorganize this later." The lowest barrier to capture means you'll actually capture everything.

Let Tags Emerge Naturally

Organization isn't gone — it's just inverted. Instead of pre-planning categories, you use tags inline (#todo, #idea, #meeting). They emerge from your natural workflow. Later, DailyNotes can surface all #meeting notes from the past month with one click.

Trust Search, Not Structure

Your brain is good at remembering context ("it was around last Tuesday") and keywords. DailyNotes gives you powerful search and calendar views. You don't need perfect organization — you need good retrieval.

Embrace Impermanence

Not every note needs to be永恒. Most notes are scaffolding — useful for a week, a project, a moment. By keeping notes in a daily stream, you naturally let old, irrelevant content fade into the archive. It's still there if you need it, but it's not cluttering your mind.

Radical Openness

Your notes, your tools, your way. DailyNotes is a hub, not a prison.

DailyNotes creates the files and generates reports. You edit them with whatever feels right today.

Sync with Your Cloud

DailyNotes stores everything in ~/Documents/DailyNotes. This means seamless sync with:

  • iCloud Drive — automatic on macOS
  • Dropbox — sync across all devices
  • Google Drive — backup to the cloud
  • Git — version control for note nerds
  • Syncthing — decentralized, private sync

Your sync strategy, your choice. DailyNotes doesn't dictate or interfere.

Supercharge your note retrieval:

The Tag System: Power in Simplicity

Organization that emerges from your writing, not imposed before it

📝 Basic Tagging: Write Naturally

Just add # before any word in your notes. No setup, no configuration:

# Meeting with Design Team
Discussed #roadmap and #UI revisions
#todo: Send mockups to stakeholders

Later, right-click the DailyNotes menu bar icon → search for #roadmap → see all roadmap discussions across months.

📅 Time-Based Reports: Your Custom Summaries

Configure any tag to generate periodic reports. Examples:

  • #todo weekly — every week's tasks in one view
  • #meeting 30 days — last month of meetings
  • #idea 1 year — your annual idea archive
  • #health 7 days — track weekly health notes

Set it once in Settings, then one-click access forever. No manual work.

🎯 Project Management: Lightweight & Powerful

Use tags as micro-project trackers:

#project-apollo Status: In Progress
#todo: Finalize API spec
#todo: Review security audit
#note: Client wants faster timeline

Generate a #project-apollo 90 days report to see the entire project timeline — notes, todos, decisions — all in context.

🧠 Knowledge Building: Connect the Dots

Tags help you see patterns over time:

  • #learning — all knowledge you've captured
  • #insight — breakthrough moments
  • #question — curiosities to explore
  • #resource — useful links and references

After months, a #learning 1 year report shows your intellectual journey. Patterns emerge you never noticed day-to-day.

👥 People & Relationships

Use @ tags to track conversations and interactions:

# Coffee with @alice
Discussed #startup idea for AI tools
#todo: Send her the investor deck

DailyNotes has a built-in @Someone report (⌘A) that surfaces all @ mentions from the past month. Never forget what you discussed.

🎨 Creative Freedom: Invent Your System

Tags are yours to define. Ideas from the community:

  • #win — celebrate small victories
  • #struggle — process hard emotions
  • #gratitude — daily appreciation practice
  • #dream — track your sleep dreams
  • #quote — collect inspiring words

There's no "right way." DailyNotes adapts to however you think.

Independent Notes: Beyond Daily Pages

Sometimes a thought needs a permanent home, not just a date stamp

📚

Create Evergreen Notes

Not everything belongs in today's stream. Some notes are reference material, project specs, or ongoing documents.

How it works:

  • Define custom note files in Settings
  • They appear in your menu bar for instant access
  • They live alongside daily notes, just not dated

Example: Personal Goals.md, Reading List.md, Project X Spec.md

🗂️

Mix Flow & Structure

The beauty of DailyNotes is choice:

  • Daily stream for spontaneous thoughts
  • Independent notes for curated knowledge
  • Both live in the same folder, searchable together

You get the benefits of流水账 (low-friction capture) AND structured reference docs. Best of both worlds.

🔄

Graduate Notes

A common workflow:

  • Start ideas in daily notes (fast, no commitment)
  • If a topic keeps coming up, create an independent note
  • Copy/consolidate related thoughts from daily stream
  • The independent note becomes your "living document"

Let structure emerge only when needed, not forced upfront.
